Crypto tracing

Can Stolen Cryptocurrency Be Traced?

Some cryptocurrency transactions can be followed through public blockchain records, but tracing is not the same as identifying a person or returning funds. The available evidence depends on the chain and the circumstances.

What can be followed?

Public ledgers may show transfers between addresses, transaction times, amounts, and interactions with certain contracts or services. A transaction hash helps others locate the same record.

What remains uncertain?

A public address is a pseudonymous identifier, not a verified identity. An exchange deposit address or service wallet may be controlled by an organization, and additional records may be required to connect activity to a person.

Why tracing can be complex

Funds may move through many addresses, services, bridges, or networks. Some data may be unavailable, and different chains expose different kinds of information.

What to preserve

Keep the original TXIDs, addresses, timestamps, exchange receipts, communications, and incident timeline. Note which facts are confirmed and which are hypotheses.

What tracing can lead to

Analysis may help prepare a report, identify services to notify, or develop investigative leads. Any freeze, seizure, or return of assets depends on lawful authority, cooperation, and case-specific facts.

How to avoid making things worse

Never share private keys or seed phrases with a tracing service. Reject guaranteed recovery promises and verify any professional's identity, terms, and credentials.
