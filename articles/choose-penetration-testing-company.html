How to Choose a Penetration Testing Company

Choosing a penetration testing provider is a risk decision as much as a technical one. Compare the scope and quality of the engagement rather than relying on bold marketing claims.

Start with a clearly defined objective

Decide whether you need an external network test, web application test, API assessment, cloud configuration review, internal test, or a broader program. Different engagements answer different questions.

Check authorization and scope

The proposal should name systems, testing windows, excluded activities, points of contact, emergency stop conditions, and the person authorized to approve the work. Third-party systems need their own permissions.

Evaluate the methodology

Ask how the team combines manual validation with automated tools, handles production risk, verifies findings, and protects data. Request a redacted example report.

Understand deliverables

A useful report explains impact, evidence, severity rationale, remediation, limitations, and retest outcomes. Confirm whether executive briefings and follow-up support are included.

Compare proposals carefully

Review assumptions, exclusions, tester qualifications, insurance where relevant, confidentiality terms, data retention, and change-control fees. Cheapest is not always best value.

Keep expectations realistic

A penetration test is a time-bounded assessment, not proof that a system is perfectly secure. Findings should be remediated and verified, and risk changes as systems change.
