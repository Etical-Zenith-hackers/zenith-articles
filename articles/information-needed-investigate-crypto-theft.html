What Information Helps Investigate a Crypto Theft?

A clear case summary helps a platform, investigator, or authority understand what is known and what remains uncertain. Gather only records you lawfully possess and store them securely.

Transaction identifiers

Record each transaction hash, network, asset, amount, sending and destination addresses, and timestamp. Check details against a reputable explorer for the relevant chain.

Exchange or wallet records

Preserve withdrawal confirmations, deposit records, account notifications, support tickets, and any transaction references supplied by the service.

Communications and websites

Save relevant messages, email headers where possible, usernames, profile links, website URLs, phone numbers, and attachments. Keep original files rather than only edited excerpts.

A timeline of events

Write down when access was lost, when transfers occurred, what actions you took, and when you contacted providers. Distinguish exact timestamps from estimates.

Account-security context

Record suspicious login alerts, password reset notifications, unknown API keys, or device changes. Do not attempt unauthorized access to someone else's account to gather more information.

Protect sensitive material

Never include seed phrases, private keys, passwords, or one-time codes. Share evidence only through verified channels and keep a record of what was shared and with whom.
