How Much Does an Ethical Hacker Cost?

There is no single reliable price for every security assessment. Cost depends on the systems involved, the agreed scope, testing depth, required expertise, timeline, and reporting needs. A useful quote makes these factors visible.

What drives the price?

  • Number and complexity of applications, hosts, APIs, or cloud environments.
  • Testing depth and whether authenticated roles or multiple user journeys must be covered.
  • Time constraints, specialist requirements, and operational risk controls.
  • Report detail, stakeholder workshops, remediation guidance, and retesting.

Compare like with like

Ask every provider to quote against the same scope and deliverables. A low headline price may exclude authenticated testing, retests, or a useful report. Compare assumptions, exclusions, credentials, experience, and how findings are validated.

Prepare before requesting a quote

List your assets, technology stack, business-critical workflows, testing window, known concerns, and any compliance requirements. Do not send production secrets or customer data merely to obtain an estimate.

Avoid false economies

A report that lists generic scanner output without validation may be less useful than a smaller, carefully scoped assessment. Conversely, no provider can promise to find every weakness or guarantee a breach will never occur.

Ask about change control

The proposal should explain what happens if scope changes, an urgent vulnerability is found, or testing must stop to protect service availability. Get any additional fees approved in writing before work expands.
