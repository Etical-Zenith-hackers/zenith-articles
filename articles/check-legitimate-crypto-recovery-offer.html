How to Check Whether a Crypto Recovery Offer Is Legitimate

After losing digital assets, it can be difficult to judge who might help. Treat recovery claims as unverified until you have independently checked the provider, written terms, methods, and limits.

Verify identity and credentials

Check the provider's legal name, business registration where relevant, contact details, and professional qualifications through independent sources. Do not rely only on testimonials or screenshots.

Ask for a written scope

A proposal should state what evidence will be reviewed, what work is excluded, expected deliverables, fees, confidentiality terms, and how the provider will handle personal data.

Reject guaranteed outcomes

Tracing can produce leads, but no analyst can guarantee that assets will be found, frozen, or returned. Ask the provider to explain uncertainty and dependencies clearly.

Never disclose wallet secrets

A public transaction hash or address is different from a seed phrase or private key. Do not give anyone wallet secrets, passwords, one-time codes, or unrestricted remote access.

Scrutinize payment demands

Be wary of unexpected fees to release assets, pay fictional taxes, or unlock a recovery tool. Verify payment terms and the recipient before sending any money.

Keep control of your accounts

Do not let a third party take over your email, exchange, or wallet. Use written, limited authorization for any legitimate technical work.
