How to Preserve Email Evidence for an Investigation

Email can contain useful information about impersonation, account compromise, fraud, or workplace incidents. Preserve original messages and avoid relying only on cropped screenshots.

Keep the original message

Use the email service's export or download feature where possible. An original message file can retain headers and metadata that screenshots omit.

Record key metadata

Note sender and recipient addresses, subject, date and time with time zone, message ID when available, and the mailbox or folder where it was found.

Preserve attachments safely

Do not open suspicious attachments on a normal work device. Follow security guidance for handling them and record filenames and relevant hashes if your approved tools provide them.

Avoid altering the source

Create a working copy for notes or redactions. Record any conversion, export, or transfer performed and preserve the original securely.

Maintain an evidence log

Document who collected the message, when it was collected, where it was stored, who received a copy, and any integrity checks performed.

Share through appropriate channels

Use the organization's approved incident process or a verified authority's reporting portal. Do not send confidential email archives to unverified third parties.
