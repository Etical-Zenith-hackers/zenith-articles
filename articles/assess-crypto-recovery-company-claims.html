How to Assess a Cryptocurrency Recovery Company's Claims

Recovery marketing often uses confident language when the facts are uncertain. Ask specific questions and independently verify answers before sharing sensitive information or paying a fee.

Ask what the service actually does

Distinguish transaction analysis, evidence organization, legal referrals, and direct control of assets. A provider should explain its role in plain language.

Ask for the basis of each claim

Request a written explanation of what evidence supports the proposed approach, what is unknown, and which steps depend on exchanges, courts, or law enforcement.

Verify the organization

Check legal identity, contact details, professional status, and relevant registrations through independent sources. Treat testimonials as claims, not proof.

Review fees and contracts

Confirm the exact deliverables, payment schedule, refund and cancellation terms, privacy practices, and any third-party costs before agreeing.

Protect credentials and devices

Do not provide seed phrases, private keys, passwords, one-time codes, or remote access. Never sign a transaction you do not understand.

Walk away from guarantees

No service can honestly promise recovery in every case. Pressure, secrecy, fabricated official partnerships, and unexplained release fees are serious red flags.
