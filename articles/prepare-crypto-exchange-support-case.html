How to Prepare for a Cryptocurrency Exchange Support Case

A concise support request helps an exchange understand the issue and route it to the right team. Use official support channels and do not send wallet secrets.

Identify the exact issue

State whether the problem is a missing deposit, withdrawal, account takeover, mistaken network, or suspected fraud. Avoid combining unrelated incidents in one ticket.

Gather transaction details

Include the asset, network, transaction hash if available, amount, destination or sending address, date and time with time zone, and the relevant deposit or withdrawal reference.

Include account references safely

Use the case form's requested account identifiers. Never include passwords, one-time codes, private keys, or seed phrases.

Build a short timeline

List the key events in order and note what steps you have already taken. Attach only relevant, readable records.

Keep case records

Save the ticket number, submission date, support replies, and any follow-up deadlines. Verify messages that claim to be from support independently.

Set realistic expectations

An exchange may be able to investigate its own records but may not be able to reverse a completed on-chain transfer or disclose another customer's private information.
