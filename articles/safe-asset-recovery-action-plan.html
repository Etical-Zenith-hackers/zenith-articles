How to Build a Safe Asset-Recovery Action Plan

A structured action plan helps prevent additional loss and directs evidence to the right channels. The correct steps depend on the asset, the transaction, and the applicable law.

Stop ongoing loss

Pause further payments, secure compromised accounts, and contact the relevant financial or platform provider through verified channels.

Define the facts

Identify the asset, proof of ownership, how it was lost, relevant dates, transaction references, and what remains unknown.

Preserve evidence

Save original messages, receipts, statements, account alerts, wallet addresses, transaction hashes, URLs, and case numbers in a secure location.

Select the appropriate reporting route

Consider banks, exchanges, platform support, insurers, law enforcement, regulators, or qualified legal counsel based on the facts and jurisdiction.

Evaluate professional help

Verify the provider's identity, lawful authority, written scope, fees, privacy terms, and realistic deliverables. Do not share account secrets or grant unnecessary access.

Track follow-up and expectations

Maintain a log of contacts and responses. Tracing may provide leads but does not guarantee an asset can be frozen or returned.
