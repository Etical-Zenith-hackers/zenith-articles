Evidence first. Permission always.

Zenith Articles promotes lawful, authorized and transparent approaches. Never access an account, device, network, wallet, or private record without the owner’s permission or other valid legal authority.

Important: Cryptocurrency and asset recovery cannot be guaranteed. Be cautious of anyone promising certain recovery, demanding large upfront fees, requesting your seed phrase/private keys, or asking you to install remote-access software without a verified reason.
